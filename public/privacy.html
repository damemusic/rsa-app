Privacy Policy

Version 2026-09-12.2 · Last updated 12 September 2026

The short version

  • We store what you write so you can get it back when you sign in.
  • When you use the AI conversation, what you write is sent to Anthropic, the company that runs the AI model, along with your profile and recent check-ins so it can respond usefully.
  • Your data is encrypted, but we hold the key, so we are technically able to read it. We don't, but we won't tell you we can't.
  • Agencies and programs that fund support services can pay us for counts — never your writing — and only if you opt in. It's off by default and the app works the same either way.
  • No agency can see your account or your check-ins — including a supervision agency, if you happen to have one.
  • You can delete your account and your data at any time.

This policy explains what RSA collects, where it goes, and what you control. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details — your email address and password. The password is handled by our authentication provider and we never see it.
  • Your check-ins — the situation you describe, the thoughts and beliefs you record, the emotions you name, and the perspective and actions you write down.
  • Your profile — your answers to the intake questions: stressors, who you turn to, what calms you, warning signs, and goals.
  • People you add — the names, relationships, relationship quality, contact frequency, and triggers you record for family members and others in your life.

Some of what you record is about other people — family members, friends, people you're in conflict with. They have not agreed to anything here. Please keep that in mind, and consider using first names or initials only.

Information collected automatically

  • Check-in timestamps — when you check in, used to show your history and streak.
  • Ordinary server logs — request times, status codes, and IP addresses, kept by our hosting providers for operating and securing the service.

We do not use advertising trackers, we do not sell your contact details, and we do not build advertising profiles.

3. Who else sees your information

Anthropic — the AI conversation

The guided conversation is powered by Claude, an AI model run by Anthropic. When you use it, we send Anthropic:

  • what you type in the conversation;
  • your profile answers;
  • the people you've added and what you recorded about them;
  • a summary of your recent check-ins.

This is what lets the AI respond to your actual situation instead of generically. If you would rather this information not leave our systems, do not use the AI conversation — the step-by-step check-in works without it.

Service providers

We use these companies to run the service. They process data on our instructions and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes:

  • Supabase — database and sign-in.
  • Railway — our application server.
  • Netlify — serving the website.

Nobody else, unless

We will disclose information if we are legally required to — a valid court order, subpoena, or warrant — or where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to prevent imminent serious harm to someone.

To be direct about one thing: we do not give your check-ins to any agency, court, program, or employer, and we do not give anyone a way to look you up. That holds whether or not you are under any kind of supervision. If you are ever told otherwise by someone claiming to act for us, it is not true.

4. About the encryption

Your check-ins and profile are encrypted in your browser before they are sent to us, and stored encrypted.

We hold the decryption key. That means we are technically capable of reading what you write. We do not do this as a matter of practice, and access is restricted — but we are telling you plainly rather than claiming a privacy guarantee we cannot make. If a court ordered us to produce your content, we would be able to.

5. The optional data program

Agencies and community programs can pay us for aggregate information about what people in their area need help with, so they can direct resources — reentry and supervision agencies among them. Participation is entirely optional and off unless you turn it on.

Because money changes hands, this counts as a sale of personal information under some state privacy laws, even though no one can be identified from what is shared. Leaving it off — or turning it off later — is how you opt out, and you never have to give a reason.

This applies to everyone who uses RSA. You do not need to be on probation or parole to use the app, and nothing here assumes you are.

What is shared if you opt in

Only three things, and only ones you pick yourself from a list after a check-in:

  • the general category the check-in was about (for example: housing, work, family);
  • who, if anyone, you turned to;
  • how it turned out.

What is never shared

  • Anything you wrote — no check-in text, ever.
  • Names, including names of people you've added.
  • Your profile answers.
  • Your email, your account, or any identifier that points to you.

How we keep counts from identifying you

  • Records are stored without your account ID.
  • Dates are reduced to the month.
  • No number is released unless at least 20 different people are behind it. If a group is smaller than that, it is withheld — and if withholding one number would let someone work it out by subtraction, we withhold a second one too.
  • Buyers receive totals for an area. They never receive records, and they cannot request information about an individual.

Changing your mind

You can turn this off whenever you like. When you do, we delete what you contributed. Totals that were already published cannot be recalled — that is the honest limit of an opt-out, and it is why the choice is opt-in.

We are not able to link these records back to you for any purpose other than deleting them at your request.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See your information — your check-ins and profile are in the app. Ask us and we'll send you a copy of everything else.
  • Correct it — edit your profile and entries in the app.
  • Delete it — delete individual check-ins in the app, or ask us to delete your account. We remove your account, profile, check-ins and any contributions within 30 days.
  • Opt out of the data program — in settings, any time. We will never make you justify it, and it will never affect your access.
  • Opt out of sale — declining or turning off the data program is how you exercise this. You do not have to create an account or give us anything extra to do it.

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights. Contact us and we will honour them. We will not treat you differently for exercising any of them.

7. How long we keep things

  • Check-ins and profile — until you delete them or close your account.
  • Contributed counts — until you opt out.
  • Server logs — a short period, set by our hosting providers, for security and troubleshooting.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to our systems is limited to people who need it to run the service. No system is perfect; if a breach affects you, we will tell you.
